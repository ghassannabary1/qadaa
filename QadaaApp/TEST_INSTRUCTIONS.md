## 📱 **Method 1: Expo Go (Best Experience)**

### Step 1: Install Expo Go
1. Open **Google Play Store** on your Android phone
2. Search **"Expo Go"**
3. Tap **Install** (free app, ~20MB)

### Step 2: Launch & Scan
1. Open **Expo Go** app
2. Tap **"Scan QR Code"** icon
3. Point camera at:
   ```
   http://localhost:8081/?turbomode=false
   ```
   *(Just copy-paste this URL into browser and scan)*

### Step 3: Test
- App loads instantly
- Tap +1 to complete prayers
- See summary cards
- Test undo functionality
- Try export/import
